# L3 · Rendering: template renditions per depth

**Tier** T1 · **Est** 1 day · **Depends on** L2 · **Phase** 4 Lenses ⭐

## Goal

Each lens produces text at its own register from the same claim set, deterministically, **with no model call**. Replaces the S4 constant card in `matchlens/rendering/__init__.py`.

## Why this ticket is the thesis

`CLAUDE.md`: *"Template prose through three diverging lenses demonstrates the entire product thesis with zero model calls."* If L3 works, MatchLens is a working product whatever happens to the agents or the model stack on the 24th. A4 later swaps templates for generation inside this same component — the contract does not move.

## ⚠️ One question the model forces

`Rendition.headline` and `body` are `str`, not optional — so **a suppressed rendition still needs both**. What goes in them is a product decision, because the producer monitor (P1) and the rejected view (P3) display these rows:

- empty strings — honest, and the monitor shows a blank card
- a non-viewer-facing note like `"(silent: below threshold)"` — useful in the monitor, but it is text in a field that means "what the viewer sees", and something will eventually render it

Ask Kehinde. Do not pick one silently, because the wrong choice puts internal text on a viewer's screen the first time a suppressed rendition leaks into the card path.

## What to build

- Real `render(moment, selected, lens) -> Rendition` with templates keyed on **moment type, signal and depth** — not on lens id (invariant 10).
- Three registers, visibly different on the same moment:
  - **casual** — plain language, consequence, no numbers. Short
  - **engaged** — the supporter register. **Its voicing is O3 and L6's — until then, render it as the match register** rather than guessing
  - **analyst** — mechanism and numbers: the value, the baseline it is measured against, the window
- `detail_level` and `allowed_claim_types` shape which claims reach the text. A claim Selection dropped must not appear.
- `surviving_claim_ids` is the selected set, and **each rendition is verified against its own surviving set**, not the moment's full set.
- Suppressed renditions carry `suppressed=True` and the reason Selection resolved, never a reason invented here.
- `language` from the lens; `en` only in v1.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The same moment through three lenses produces **three materially different texts** — assert they differ, and that the analyst one contains a number while the casual one does not
- [ ] **No claim appears in text that Selection did not select.** Assert against `surviving_claim_ids`, which is the structural version of the same check
- [ ] Deterministic: same moment, claims and lens, byte-identical text. No RNG, no timestamp in the body
- [ ] A suppressed rendition is emitted with `suppressed=True`, exactly one reason, and no asserted claim in its text (invariant 5 — nothing with no evidence behind it may assert anything)
- [ ] Templates are keyed on type, signal and depth; no lens id literal in the module (invariant 10)
- [ ] **Never the string "xG"** anywhere — in template, comment or test. `shot_quality` (invariant 12). A test on the source text is cheap and catches it permanently
- [ ] Every number in a rendition traces to a claim's value, not recomputed in the template
- [ ] `calltrace.entered("Rendering")` still fires

## Definition of done

Criteria pass, green, status updated.

## Do not

- Do not call a model. L1 of the ladder is model-free by definition, and A4 is where generation lands.
- Do not re-filter claims. Selection already decided; filtering again here means two places own one rule.
- Do not compute a value. If a number is not on a claim, it cannot be said.
- Do not implement the partisan voice — L6, and it is blocked on O3.
