# D3 · Speed control for the judge path

**Tier** T1 · **Est** 0.5 day · **Depends on** D1, F4 · **Phase** 5 Delivery
**Status:** done 24 Oct. **J1: a full match (seed 1) takes 227.4 s at 25×, 3 min 47 s, on real time.** How kickoff and speed work is D33.

## Goal

A judge opens the deployed URL and watches a **full match end to end in under five minutes**, with ordering and relative timing intact (J1).

## Most of this already exists

- `F4` is done: the replay harness takes any speed, and `FAST_SPEED = 25.0`
- `MATCHLENS_REPLAY_SPEED` is already an env var on the deployed container app, set to `25`
- D23 settled that a viewer's request wakes the container, which starts the match

So D3 is not the mechanism — it is the **judge-facing control and the judge-facing honesty**: a way to pick a speed without redeploying, and a page that says what it is doing.

## ⚠️ The thing that makes this a T1 ticket rather than a config value

A judge arriving mid-match sees a match already in progress with no explanation, or waits for a cold start with no feedback. Either reads as broken. X4 raises min replicas to 1 for the judging window, which fixes the cold start, but the page still has to say **where in the match we are and how fast it is running** — otherwise the three panels look like they are missing cards rather than replaying minute 12.

## What to build

- Speed selectable without a redeploy — a query parameter or a control on the page, defaulting to the configured value.
- The page states the current match clock and the speed, visibly.
- Restart-from-kickoff, so a judge who arrives at minute 80 can see the match from the start rather than its last ten minutes. This is the single most useful thing in the ticket.
- Offsets stay in **match-clock milliseconds** regardless of speed — a 45s offset at 25× passes in under two seconds of real time, and D1's rule must still hold exactly.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A full match completes end to end in **under five minutes** at the demo speed (J1), with every card arriving in `display_at_clock` order
- [x] Relative timing survives: the gap between two cards scales with the speed and does not collapse to zero or drift
- [x] Speed is changeable without a redeploy, and an invalid value is refused rather than silently defaulting
- [x] The page shows the match clock and the speed at all times
- [x] A fresh visitor can start from kickoff
- [x] D1's release rule still holds at every speed, including 1× and the demo speed — assert at both, since a bug here only appears at one of them

## Outcome

- **J1** — 227.4 s on real time at 25× (`pytest -m slow tests/delivery/test_playback.py`). On virtual time the suite checks a full match finishes in under five minutes, within a second of `duration / speed`.
- **Order and relative timing** — every card in `display_at_clock` order; the wall gap between two cards is the match gap over the speed, within two match seconds.
- **Speed without a redeploy** — `POST /playback {"speed": x}`, and the page offers 1×, 5×, 25× and 50×. One speed for the match, for everyone watching (D33). `0`, negatives, above `max_replay_speed` (100) and non-numbers are refused with 422 and the speed is left as it was. A change mid-match is taken up at once and skips or repeats nothing. Live: 24.9 match s per s at 25×, 5.0 after switching to 5×.
- **Clock and speed on the page** — every view's header shows the speed and the speed control; the clock is the viewer's own (D32).
- **From kickoff** — "Watch from kickoff" makes the tab's sessions as far behind live as the match has run; the button becomes "Back to live". Delivery keeps every card, so a new session is given the cards its video has not reached (D33).
- **D1's rule at 1× and at 25×** — asserted at both, on virtual time: never early, always in order.

## Definition of done

Criteria pass, green, status updated. **Time the full run and record the number**, because J1 is a stated figure and the submission will quote it.

## Do not

- Do not compress stopped time to hit the five minutes. That changes what a viewer sees and needs its own decision — cut-ladder note on F4/J1 covers the arithmetic, and 25× already clears the bar.
- Do not scale `stream_offset_ms` with the speed. It is match time.
- Do not use wall clock for anything the release rule reads.
