# X1 · Recap per lens

**Tier** T3 · **Est** 1 day, less if merged · **Depends on** A3 · **Phase** 8 Submission

## ⚠️ Its tier understates it: the brief names recaps twice

The challenge asks for *"automated match recaps"* in its opening paragraph **and** again under narrative generation (iii). It is T3 and on the cut ladder, which was the right call when it was judged on product value alone. Against the brief it is a named requirement with no other answer in the build.

## ⚠️ And it is the same mechanism as D4 (D34)

A summary of 0→68 for a late joiner and a recap of 0→full-time at the whistle are **one function with a different `to_clock`.** Built once, both the catch-up ticket and the recap requirement are answered — and **the merge removes the dependency on A3**, because a recap assembled from the moment and claim log does not need the verifier to exist.

So the realistic shape is: build the shared summariser, point it at full time, and D4 falls out for free.

## Goal

At the whistle, each lens produces a recap of the match **in its own register** — from the same moments, like every other card.

## Why it demos well

Three different recaps of one match, side by side, is the thesis restated at the end: same understanding, three experiences. It is also the only artefact that shows accumulation across the whole match rather than a moment at a time, which answers the brief's *"key narratives"* better than anything else available.

## What to build

- `catchup.summarise(lens, from_clock, to_clock)` — the shared function. Full time is `to_clock = final whistle`.
- Per lens, from that lens's moments and surviving claims: casual gets the goals and the result; analyst gets the shape of the match in numbers; the team lens gets it as consequence for its side (D31, supporter-oriented).
- **Every statement traces to a stored moment or claim.** A recap is the place most likely to drift into unsupported summary, and invariant 5 applies to it exactly as to a card.
- Template-driven, like L3. A model version is A4's business, not this ticket's.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] One recap per lens at full time, materially different from each other
- [ ] Every figure and claim in a recap traces to a stored row — assert it, do not trust it
- [ ] The same function with an earlier `to_clock` produces a mid-match summary (this is D4, for free)
- [ ] A lens that showed almost nothing produces a short recap, not an invented one
- [ ] No model call needed
- [ ] Deterministic on the demo seed

## Do not

- Do not write a recap that asserts what no claim supports.
- Do not build it twice — one summariser, two clocks (D34).
- Do not wait for A3. Merged, it does not need it.
