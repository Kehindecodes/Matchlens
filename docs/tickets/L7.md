# L7 · Deterministic claim builder: D29's metrics from the record, no model

**Tier** T1 · **Est** 1.5 days · **Depends on** L3, M1b, D29 · **Phase** 4 Lenses ⭐

## Goal

Every moment carries real claims, so every card can say **why**, with no model call. Replaces the S4 stub in `matchlens/explanation/__init__.py`, whose only claim is `metric="triggers"` (Q25 part 3).

## Why this ticket exists

The build-order rule puts the thesis on L1: templates, no LLM. Until now the only claims came from a stub, so L4's panels differed in how much they said but not in what they said (Q25). A2's agent was meant to supply real claims; it has not started and 20 October has passed. This ticket gives the L1 path real claims of its own. A2, if it lands, emits claims in the same vocabulary on top, and A5 falls back to this builder when the model is unavailable.

## What to build

- **`Metric`**, a closed enum of D29's eight: `goals`, `shot_quality`, `chances`, `build_up_passes`, `territory`, `possession_control`, `pressing_intensity`, `chance_quality`. `Operand.metric` takes it in place of `str` (D21 said "a string until U1–U3"; D14 says closed sets are enums). `triggers` goes.
- **The builder**: `explain(moment, frozen_log)` keeps its signature and returns D29's claims for the moment type:
  - **goal**: `goals` with the scorer as subject, the score after it, `build_up_passes`, `shot_quality`
  - **chance**: `shot_quality`, `chances` over the last 10 min
  - **tactical shift**: the signal that fired, comparative against the same team earlier and against the opponent; interpretive with `backing_reading` and the threshold the wording implies (1.25 plain, 2.0 strong, D29)
- Every operand cites its evidence: events, readings, or the possession's events. Between them the claims cite **every trigger** of the moment (D22's `coverage`). A claim that cannot cite is not emitted (invariant 5).
- Reads only the frozen log and derived understanding (possessions, readings, epochs), never `Frame` (invariant 1).
- `model="deterministic"` and a `model_version` that is bumped by hand when a rule changes (invariant 7).
- **Rendering**: fact rows in `matchlens/rendering/templates.json` for each metric at each depth, built on D29's wording rules, and player names alongside team names (from the match setup, as L3 does for teams). Casual rows carry no numbers. Goal rows handle a low `shot_quality` ("from a hard chance"), since every goal on seed 1 came from one (D29).

## Settled before starting (23 Oct)

- **"Earlier" in a comparative claim** is the same signal's last reading for the same team in the previous epoch, so the text can say "since the equaliser". In the opening epoch there is none, and only the comparison against the opponent is made.
- **Unmarked.** Claude Code implements it, tests included.

## Out of scope

- **Q25 parts 1 and 2**: which moments casual sees, and whether `minimal` states a claim. `_CLAIMS_SPOKEN` stays `MINIMAL: 0` until they are answered, so casual text changes only through its headline and body rows.
- A2's agent and A3's real verifier. The stub verifier still passes every claim.
- Player scope. Naming the scorer is a match-scope card naming a player (D29).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `Operand.metric` is the `Metric` enum; nothing emits `triggers`
- [ ] Every goal, chance and tactical-shift moment on seed 1 gets at least one claim, and every claim's value recomputes from its cited evidence (a test recounts each metric from the cited rows)
- [ ] Every trigger of every moment appears in the evidence of at least one of its claims
- [ ] A goal claim names the scorer, and the goal card shows the scorer's name and no player id
- [ ] "A quick attack", "a patient move", "a big chance", "third big chance in ten minutes" and the strong interpretive words appear only when D29's rule holds (a test per rule, true and false)
- [ ] Casual cards still carry no numbers; analyst cards carry a number from a claim, never the trigger count
- [ ] No claim reads a `Frame` (the existing boundary test still covers the explainer)
- [ ] Deterministic: same seed, byte-identical claims and card text
- [ ] Holds across the 12 matches D29 was checked on (four archetypes × seeds 1–3): no exception, every moment explained
- [ ] The word for shot quality is never the other one (invariant 12)

## Definition of done

Criteria pass, green, status updated. Re-take L4's screenshot (`docs/screenshots/L4-silent-panel.jpg`) on seed 1 at the same clock, so the demo shot shows cards that say why.

## Do not

- Do not lower or tune any D29 threshold per match. They are standard values.
- Do not add a metric beyond the eight. A new one is a change to D29.
- Do not let the builder decide which lens sees a claim. That is Selection's.
