# X4 · Judging-window hardening

**Tier** T1 · **Est** 0.5 day · **Depends on** S5 · **Phase** 8 Submission

## Goal

The deployed URL works, every time, for anyone, from now until **10 November** — when judging ends, not when submission closes.

## ⚠️ Size the cost before setting min replicas to 1

`main.bicep` has `minReplicas: 0 // scale to zero until X4 sets min 1 for judging`. Setting it to 1 removes the cold start, which is the right call — a judge who waits through a container boot and a match regenerating from kickoff has already formed a view.

**But it also means the container bills continuously for about sixteen days**, against the `matchlens-monthly` budget of 15 in the billing currency with alerts at 50, 80 and 100 per cent. One always-on Consumption replica for two weeks is plausibly most of that budget or past it.

**Check the number before flipping the flag**, and if it does not fit, the options in order: a smaller CPU/memory allocation on the app, min-1 only for a judging window you set by date, or accepting scale-to-zero and documenting the first-load delay in the README as D23 already asks. **Running out of budget mid-judging is worse than a cold start** — the URL simply stops.

## What to build

- `minReplicas: 1`, once the cost is sized.
- **Rate limit the public endpoints.** One replica, a public URL and an unbounded SSE endpoint is a resource anyone can exhaust by accident.
- **Cached demo renditions**, which overlaps D36's pre-computed A2 claims — one cache serves both the latency budget and this ticket.
- **Confirm the budget alert is live** and that its email reaches somewhere you will read before 10 November (J2).
- Confirm the deploy identity's role assignments survive, since `infra/README.md` warns they sit on the group and the registry and are lost if the group is deleted.

## The test that matters most

**Hand the URL to someone who has never seen the project** and watch them open it on a phone and a laptop without being told anything. If they cannot tell what they are looking at within fifteen seconds, that is X3's problem, but if it does not load, or loads mid-match with no clock, or shows three empty panels, that is this ticket's.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Cost of min-1 through 10 November is calculated and fits the budget, or min-1 is not set and the delay is documented instead
- [ ] A cold browser reaches a working match with no explanation
- [ ] The public endpoints are rate limited, and the limit is tested rather than assumed
- [ ] The budget alert is confirmed live, with a recipient that will be read
- [ ] A match can be restarted from kickoff by a visitor arriving late (D33)
- [ ] It works on a phone-width screen
- [ ] The URL is in the submission and resolves from outside your network
- [ ] A second visitor at the same time does not disturb the first

## Definition of done

Criteria pass, status updated. **Record the measured cold-start and full-replay times in the README** — X3 quotes them, and a measured number beats an estimate.

## Do not

- Do not set min-1 without sizing it against the budget.
- Do not leave an unbounded public SSE endpoint on a single replica.
- Do not delete and recreate the resource group this week. The role assignments go with it, and `infra/README.md` has the recovery steps for a reason.
