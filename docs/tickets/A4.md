# A4 · Lens-aware generation from each lens's surviving claim set

**Tier** T2 · **Est** 1 day · **Depends on** A3, L3 · **Phase** 6 Agents

## Goal

L0 rendering: a model writes each lens's card from **that lens's surviving claims**, in that lens's register.

## Where it sits

Every model call in the system lives in Rendering (D17), and this is that call. L3's templates are the rung below; A4 replaces them at L0 and A5 drops back to them when the model cannot deliver.

**Verified per rendition, not per moment:** each rendition is checked against its own `surviving_claim_ids`, not the moment's full claim set. A lens that kept three of seven claims is accountable for three.

## What to build

- A generation path in `matchlens/rendering/` behind the existing `render()` signature.
- The three registers from L3 and D31 — casual plain, analyst mechanism and numbers, team **supporter-oriented** (D31: oriented, not voiced).
- The prompt receives the surviving claims and **nothing else**: no moment internals, no importance score, no other lens's set.
- `detail_level` and `allowed_claim_types` still shape what may appear, exactly as in L3.

## ⚠️ The discipline the verifier cannot enforce for you

D22 settled `coverage` as **claim-side** completeness, and the text-side reading — *the sentence asserts nothing the claims do not support* — was ruled out because Verification sits before Rendering and there is no text to check at that point.

So nothing downstream will catch A4 adding an assertion. That makes it this ticket's own responsibility, by test: generate, then assert every number and every named entity in the output traces to a surviving claim. A model that adds "their best spell of the half" where no claim says so has broken the central promise, and the architecture will not notice.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each lens's text is generated from its own surviving set, and a claim the lens dropped never appears
- [ ] **Every number and named entity in the output traces to a claim.** The test that stands in for the check that cannot exist
- [ ] Three lenses on one moment read as three registers, and the team lens is supporter-oriented rather than voiced (D31)
- [ ] Never the string "xG" (invariant 12)
- [ ] A model failure leaves L3's template text in place rather than an empty card — the seam A5 uses
- [ ] `model` and `model_version` travel with the rendition's provenance
- [ ] Output is deterministic enough to demo: same claims and lens, the same card within an acceptable variance, or the demo seed pinned

## Do not

- Do not pass the model anything beyond the surviving claims.
- Do not let the model restate a number it was not given.
- Do not remove L3's templates. They are the rung below.
