# D4 · Catch-up on join, per lens, verified claims only

**Tier** T2 · **Est** 1 day · **Depends on** D1, M1 · **Phase** 5 Delivery

## Goal

A viewer joining at the 68th minute gets **only what their lens would have shown**, and a team-lens joiner is caught up from their side's point of view (V9).

## The seam already exists

`ReleaseQueues._catch_up` calls `catchup.summarise(lens, 0, position)` today and **throws the result away**, with a comment saying the summary is shown once D4 exists. So the hook, the per-session first-join detection and the lens lookup are already in place. D4 fills in the summary and shows it.

## ⚠️ This is panel-only, and smaller than it looks (D34)

Catch-up needs a surface with **history**. The companion panel has one; an overlay does not — there is nowhere to put a summary on a transient graphic. So this is a panel feature, with at most a single "match so far" overlay on join.

And D1 already solves most of it: the release rule is keyed on the **viewer's own position**, so a start-over viewer beginning at kickoff forty minutes late gets every card at the right moment with no catch-up at all. **This ticket is only for someone joining live and staying live.**

**It is also the same mechanism as X1.** A summary of 0→68 and a full-time recap are one function with a different `to_clock`. Build it once, satisfy both, and X1 stops needing A3 — which is what makes the brief's *"automated match recaps"* reachable.

## ⚠️ Read the tier honestly before starting

This is **T2 and the first thing in Phase 5 to cut.** It is also what makes L5's lens switch feel complete — switching to the analyst lens mid-match currently shows only subsequent cards, and this is the ticket that would show what you missed. Worth knowing which it is before spending a day on it with two left.

One more thing: *"verified claims only"* assumes verification exists. With no agents (O6), everything is template prose off deterministic moments and nothing has been through A3 — so that phrase is vacuous unless A3 lands. If the agents are cut, the criterion becomes "claims that survived Selection", which is weaker and should be said rather than quietly substituted.

## What to build

- `catchup.summarise(lens, from_clock, to_clock)` producing a per-lens summary of the window before the viewer joined.
- **It is a summary, never a replay.** The viewer does not receive the cards they missed — that would violate invariant 8 in the other direction, dumping forty minutes of cards at once.
- ~~Per lens: a casual joiner gets the goals and nothing else; an analyst joiner gets the tactical shape of the half; a team-lens joiner gets it told from their side.~~ **Q30:** every lens is told the goals, with how each was scored, in its own words and from its side. With no goals yet, it is told the 3 most important moments it showed.
- Shown once, on first release for that session — the `_joined` set already tracks this.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A 68th-minute joiner receives a summary covering 0–68, not the cards themselves. *A viewer 45 s behind is summarised to 67:15, where their video is.*
- [x] The summary differs by lens on the same match and join clock — three joins, three summaries. *Every lens is told the same goals (Q30), in its own depth's words.*
- [x] A team-lens joiner's summary is told from their side (consequence, not mechanism). *Sided headlines, and "Level at 1–1" before the scorer (D31).*
- [x] It is shown exactly once per session, and a reconnect does not repeat it. *It is the first entry in the session's log, so `Last-Event-ID` counts it like a card.*
- [x] No card that was already released is re-sent, and no card from before the join is released at all. *The cards are split at the join, at one position.*
- [x] Every statement in a summary traces to a moment or claim from the window — nothing invented to fill a gap (invariant 5). *The claims survived Selection, not verification: A3 is a stub.*
- [x] A viewer joining at kickoff gets no summary, not an empty one. *Nor does a viewer whose lens has shown nothing yet.*

## Definition of done

Criteria pass, green, status updated.

## Do not

- Do not replay missed cards. Summarise.
- Do not release anything whose `display_at_clock` is behind the viewer's position.
- Do not claim "verified" if A3 does not exist. Say what the claims actually survived.
