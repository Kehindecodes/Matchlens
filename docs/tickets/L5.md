# L5 · Lens switching mid-match

**Tier** T1 · **Est** 0.5 day · **Depends on** L4 · **Phase** 4 Lenses ⭐

## Goal

A viewer switches lens and it takes effect on the next card, with no reload, showing what that lens **would have shown** (V6).

## Why it is worth half a day

It is the cheapest possible proof that the lenses are configuration rather than three separate products. A judge who switches from casual to analyst mid-match and immediately sees denser cards has understood the thesis without being told it — and it costs one POST and a session update.

## ⚠️ "Would have shown" needs reading carefully

It does **not** mean replaying that lens's whole history from kickoff. The session has `last_seen_clock`, and back-filling what was missed is **Catch-up (D4), which is T2** and a different ticket.

So v1 is: **the switch affects subsequent cards only.** Cards already on screen stay as they are. That is honest, demonstrable, and does not pretend to a feature that is not built. If the panel should clear on switch rather than keep the old lens's cards, that changes what a viewer sees — ask.

## What to build

- An endpoint that changes `lens_id` on a `ViewerSession`. A low-frequency POST, which is exactly why the stack is SSE and not WebSocket (D5).
- `ViewerSession` is frozen, so a switch **replaces the session with an updated copy** — the model's docstring already says a session that moves on is replaced.
- The SSE connection stays open. No reload, no reconnect, no `Last-Event-ID` replay.
- The next release for that session uses the new lens: Selection and Rendering are given the new `Lens` row, and nothing else in the pipeline knows a switch happened.
- Validate the pairing: `focus_team` is required iff the new lens is team scope. The `Lens` validator covers the lens; the session's own `focus_team` has to agree, and `ViewerSession`'s docstring notes that rule is checked **where the session meets its lens** — which is here.

**Built as a viewer page, `/watch`: one panel with a lens picker (24 Oct). The three-panel demo view at `/` is unchanged.** Cards already shown stay; a marker in the list says where the new lens takes over.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Switching mid-match changes subsequent cards without a reload or a dropped SSE connection
- [x] The next card after a switch is the new lens's rendition of the next moment, not a re-render of the last one
- [x] Switching to `team_engaged` requires a `focus_team`; switching away from it clears it. Assert both directions
- [x] A switch to an unknown `lens_id` is refused and the session is unchanged
- [x] No card already released is retracted, duplicated or reordered (invariant 8)
- [x] Switching twice in quick succession leaves the session on the second lens, with no card from the first in between
- [x] The pipeline is untouched: a switch produces no new moment, claim or reading

## Definition of done

Criteria pass, green, status updated.

## Do not

- Do not back-fill the new lens's missed cards. That is Catch-up (D4), T2.
- Do not reconnect the stream or reload the page.
- Do not re-render past moments. Renditions are append-only records of what was shown.
