# P4 · Archetype regression check in CI

**Tier** T2 · **Est** 1 day · **Depends on** R2, U3 · **Phase** 7 Producer
**📝 Walkthrough required** (D29) — `docs/walkthroughs/P4.md`.

## Goal

Every signal, across all four archetypes, with readings asserted in range — on every commit.

## What it actually protects

Co-adaptation. From the data model: *"signals must be checked against several archetypes, or the simulator and the signals quietly co-adapt until the signals only work on the matches the generator happens to make."*

And it is the **evidence for the production claim.** D25 settled that archetypes never run in production — they are *"the test fixture that justifies the claim that the signals would work on a real feed."* P4 is where that justification is mechanical rather than asserted, which is the argument a platform actually wants.

## What to build

- A CI job running all four archetypes across several seeds, asserting each signal's readings fall in a documented range.
- **Carry U3's key assertion into CI:** `pressing_intensity` dissociates from `possession_control` on at least one archetype. U3 proves it once at build time; here it is proved on every commit, which is the difference between a property and a coincidence.
- Assert the *shape* of each archetype survives: card counts ordered casual < team < analyst, which D30 recorded as holding across twelve generated matches.
- Fast enough to stay in CI. The suite budget is two minutes; if this cannot fit, run fewer seeds rather than moving it to a nightly nobody reads.

## ⚠️ Honest note on timing

This is an **argument, not a demo.** It makes nothing visible to a judge and it is T2. On the last build day it loses to X3 on every measure. Worth building after submission, when it protects the thing it was designed to protect — which is the next change to a signal, not this week's.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All four archetypes × several seeds, every v1 signal, readings in documented ranges
- [ ] Pressing dissociates from possession on at least one archetype, asserted here
- [ ] Card counts stay ordered casual < team < analyst on every archetype
- [ ] Runs on every commit, inside the suite's two-minute budget
- [ ] A deliberately broken signal fails the job — assert the check can fail, or it is decoration

## Do not

- Do not tune a signal to make this pass. That is the co-adaptation it exists to catch, and D25 says so.
- Do not move it out of CI to make it bigger.
