# P2 · Mute circuit breaker, ProducerAction log, viewer notice

**Tier** T2 · **Est** 1 day · **Depends on** D1 · **Phase** 7 Producer

## Goal

A producer can stop a lens mid-match, the intervention is logged, and viewers who were getting cards are told.

## Why it exists

It answers the buyer's only real objection. From Positioning: *"the producer features are not a second product. They are the answer to the buyer's only real objection, which is an AI saying things live, unsupervised, on their platform. Suppress-and-release and the verification log exist because nobody deploys something they cannot stop."*

## The seam is already wired

`control.is_muted(lens_id, at_clock)` returns `False` today, and **`CardRouter.receive` already calls it and drops the card.** `control.mute()` raises `NotImplementedError("the mute circuit breaker lands in P2")`.

So the hook is in the right place — and crucially it is **before** `enqueue`, which gives you the first property below for free.

## Three properties, and the third is the subtle one

1. **Mute applies to later cards only and never delays what is queued.** Already true: the router drops at receive, so cards already in a session's release queue still release on their own clocks. Do not add a queue sweep — invariant 8 is about delay, and retracting a queued card would be a new behaviour nobody asked for.
2. **One lens mutes without the others.** Mute is keyed on `lens_id`.
3. **A viewer who had cards is told; a viewer who had none stays quiet.** Telling someone "insights paused" when they have never seen an insight is confusing — it announces the existence of a feature by announcing its absence. Use the session's `last_seen_clock`, which is `None` until the first card is released: that field already distinguishes the two cases.

## What to build

- A mute store keyed by `lens_id` with the clock it took effect, and a real `is_muted`.
- A **`ProducerAction` row per intervention** — `target_id` as a `Ref` to the lens, `action`, `actor`, `clock_ms`, `reason`. The model's validator already restricts targets to a moment, rendition or lens.
- Unmute, because a circuit breaker that cannot be reset is a kill switch.
- The viewer notice, shown only to sessions with a non-null `last_seen_clock`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Muting a lens stops subsequent cards for it and **nothing else** — other lenses unaffected, assert directly
- [ ] Cards already queued for a session still release at their own times
- [ ] A session with `last_seen_clock = None` gets **no** notice; one with a clock gets it
- [ ] Every mute and unmute writes a `ProducerAction` with an actor and a reason
- [ ] Unmute resumes cards from that clock on, and does not back-fill what was muted
- [ ] `control.mute()` no longer raises
- [ ] A mute mid-demo is visible on the producer view and does not disturb the clock

## Do not

- Do not sweep or retract queued cards.
- Do not back-fill on unmute. Muted cards are gone, and that is what the producer asked for.
- Do not let a viewer mute anything.
