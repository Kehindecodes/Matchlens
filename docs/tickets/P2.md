# P2 · Mute circuit breaker, ProducerAction log, viewer notice

**Tier** T2 · **Est** 1 day · **Depends on** D1 · **Phase** 7 Producer

## Goal

A producer can stop a lens mid-match, the intervention is logged, and viewers who were getting cards are told.

## Why it exists

It answers the buyer's only real objection. From Positioning: *"the producer features are not a second product. They are the answer to the buyer's only real objection, which is an AI saying things live, unsupervised, on their platform. Suppress-and-release and the verification log exist because nobody deploys something they cannot stop."*

## The seam is already wired

`control.is_muted(lens_id, at_clock)` returns `False` today, and **`CardRouter.receive` already calls it and drops the card.** `control.mute()` raises `NotImplementedError("the mute circuit breaker lands in P2")`.

So the hook is in the right place — and crucially it is **before** `enqueue`, which gives you the first property below for free.

## Three properties, and the third is the subtle one

1. **Mute applies to later cards only and never delays what is queued.** Already true: the router drops at receive, so cards already in a session's release queue still release on their own clocks. Do not add a queue sweep — invariant 8 is about delay, and retracting a queued card would be a new behaviour nobody asked for.
2. **One lens mutes without the others.** Mute is keyed on `lens_id`.
3. **A viewer who had cards is told; a viewer who had none stays quiet.** Telling someone "insights paused" when they have never seen an insight is confusing — it announces the existence of a feature by announcing its absence. Use the session's `last_seen_clock`, which is `None` until the first card is released: that field already distinguishes the two cases.

## What to build

- A mute store keyed by `lens_id` with the clock it took effect, and a real `is_muted`.
- A **`ProducerAction` row per intervention** — `target_id` as a `Ref` to the lens, `action`, `actor`, `clock_ms`, `reason`. The model's validator already restricts targets to a moment, rendition or lens.
- Unmute, because a circuit breaker that cannot be reset is a kill switch.
- The viewer notice, shown only to sessions with a non-null `last_seen_clock`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Muting a lens stops subsequent cards for it and **nothing else** — other lenses unaffected, assert directly
  - `test_muting_a_lens_stops_its_later_cards_and_nothing_else`, `test_a_muted_lens_has_a_muted_rendition_for_every_moment_inside_the_mute` (other lenses' cards identical)
- [x] Cards already queued for a session still release at their own times
  - `test_cards_already_queued_still_release_at_their_own_times`
- [x] A session with `last_seen_clock = None` gets **no** notice; one with a clock gets it
  - `test_a_viewer_who_had_no_cards_is_not_told`, `test_a_viewer_who_had_cards_is_told_when_their_video_reaches_the_mute`
- [x] Every mute and unmute writes a `ProducerAction` with an actor and a reason
- [x] Unmute resumes cards from that clock on, and does not back-fill what was muted
- [x] `control.mute()` no longer raises
  - `Mutes.mute`; the stub functions are gone and S1's component test names `Mutes.mute` and `Mutes.is_muted`
- [x] A mute mid-demo is visible on the producer view and does not disturb the clock
  - `test_a_mute_does_not_disturb_the_match_clock`; screenshot `docs/screenshots/P2-muted-lens.jpg`

## Do not

- Do not sweep or retract queued cards.
- Do not back-fill on unmute. Muted cards are gone, and that is what the producer asked for.
- Do not let a viewer mute anything.

## As built (Q44: 1A, 2B, 3A, 4A)

- **Control.** `matchlens/control/mutes.py`: `Mutes` keeps each lens's mutes as stretches of the match clock, and every mute and unmute as a `ProducerAction` (`suppress` and `release`, target a lens `Ref`). `is_muted` is the clock check, `stops` is that or "muted now", `in_force` names the mute stopping a lens. `Notice` is the stream message for a viewer.
- **Recorded (1A).** `Pipeline` asks `Mutes.stops` for each lens before it selects, and `select(..., muted=True)` returns the `muted` reason ahead of every other, so a muted lens gets a suppressed rendition, spends none of its rate limit, and the producer view reads it. The router's drop stays as the backstop for a card already past Selection when the click lands; that one is logged, not recorded.
- **Arrival (2B).** A card is stopped if its moment is inside a mute or the lens is muted when the card arrives. A moment inside a mute that arrives after the unmute stays stopped.
- **The notice (3A).** `CardRouter.notify` holds a `Notice` in every session's release queue at the mute's clock. When it comes due it goes into the log, and so counts for `Last-Event-ID`, only if the viewer had cards by then (`last_seen_clock`, or a card released earlier in the same batch). An unmute is told only to a viewer who was told of the pause. The stream sends it as a `notice` event and `panel.js` draws a line in the card list: "Insights paused · from 51:09", "Insights resumed · from 60:00".
- **The controls (4A).** `POST /producer/mute`, `POST /producer/unmute` and `GET /producer/actions` exist only with the producer view. The page has a reason box (required, refused by the page before any request) and a Mute or Unmute button per lens, an "Acting as" box defaulting to `producer`, and the log of interventions. The feed shows a muted lens as **Muted by a producer**, with who, when and why.
- **Not done here.** A viewer's idle line still says "quiet for N min by choice" while a lens is paused; the notice line is what explains it. A mute is not persisted: it lives and dies with the delivery process, like the sessions.
