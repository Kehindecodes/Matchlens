# L6 · Team scope: importance reweight and partisan framing

**Tier** T1 · **Est** 0.5 day · **Depends on** L2, L3 · **Phase** 4 Lenses ⭐

## ⚠️ Blocked on O3 — do not start the framing half

**O3 · Team lens register — supporter-voiced or supporter-oriented.** Unresolved, and it decides the words:

- **supporter-voiced** — *"we are level"*, *"that saved us"*. What the prototype used
- **supporter-oriented** — *"Fulham are level, which is the result they wanted"*. Partisan in selection and emphasis, neutral in voice

The objection on record: the Features page once listed a club-allegiance lens under *explicitly not building*, and D1 outweighed that objection rather than answering it — **a platform may not want to ship something that sounds like it takes a side.** That is a product and sales judgement, so it is Kehinde's.

The **reweight** half does not depend on O3 and can be built now.

## Goal

The team lens reaches the *scope* axis — the second axis the whole three-lens design exists to show — **with no new entities.**

## Why it needs nothing new

Every signal is already per-team, so the reweight is arithmetic over data that exists. From D1: team scope *"reaches the same axis for the cost of a rendering register"*. That is the entire reason player scope was deferred and this was kept.

## What to build

**Half one — the reweight (Selection, L2's team path):**
- Boost moments affecting the result for the chosen side.
- Drop neutral tactical curiosities that the analyst lens keeps.
- **Keep opponent moments.** Under D2 a moment about the opponent is *maximally* relevant to a supporter — an opponent goal is the most important thing that can happen to you. A team lens that filters by subject has been built as player scope on the wrong axis, and this is the single most likely way to get L6 wrong.

**Half two — the framing (Rendering, L3's register), after O3:**
- Tell the moment as **consequence for the chosen side** rather than mechanism. The analyst lens says what happened; the team lens says what it means for them.
- The same underlying claims, a different sentence. No claim appears that Selection did not select.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **V11 holds:** switching `focus_team` reframes later cards **without changing which events exist.** Run the same match with the lens pointed at each side and assert the moment set is near-identical while the texts differ. This is the criterion that proves reweight-and-reframe rather than filter
- [ ] An opponent goal appears in the team lens, with high importance
- [ ] A neutral tactical curiosity the analyst lens shows is dropped by the team lens
- [ ] The team lens's card count on the demo match sits between casual's and analyst's — if it matches either exactly, the reweight is doing nothing
- [ ] No new entity, field or enum value was added
- [ ] The framing half is absent and said to be absent until O3 is settled
- [ ] `focus_team` drives everything; no club name appears as a literal anywhere (invariant 10)

## Definition of done

Criteria pass, green, status updated. If only the reweight landed, say so in the status rather than marking the ticket done.

## Do not

- Do not filter by subject. Reweight and reframe (D2).
- Do not guess O3. Build the reweight, leave the register as the match register, and say so.
- Do not add a `Run`, a new scope, or a fourth lens.
- Do not hardcode a team name or a lens id.
