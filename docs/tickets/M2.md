## Goal

The same tactical shift does not fire three times in four minutes.

## Why it is a separate ticket from M1

M1 decides *whether this reading is notable*. M2 decides *whether we have already said this*. The first is a judgement about the match; the second is a judgement about **our own recent output**, which means it is the first component holding memory of what MatchLens has said rather than of what happened. Different state, different failure mode, different test.

It is also the ticket that makes the product bearable. A spotter with no cooldown is correct and unwatchable: territory drifts across a threshold, fires, drifts back, fires again. The viewer sees three cards saying the same thing and concludes the system is noisy — which is the exact impression the whole design exists to avoid.

## What to build

- Suppression inside `matchlens/spotting/`, applied after the spotter judges and before a `Moment` is emitted.
- **Cooldown** per `(signal, team, type)`: once fired, that combination cannot fire again for a configured window. In config, not inline.
- **Duplicate detection**: a second crossing of the same threshold in the same direction, while the first is still the current state, is the same event — not a new one. The signal recrossing a boundary it never meaningfully left is the common case.
- **Goals are never suppressed.** A goal is a goal however recently anything else fired. Whatever the cooldown table says, an `Outcome.GOAL` trigger passes.
- Keep it deterministic and keep the state per match, so two matches in one process cannot suppress each other's moments.

## The distinction worth getting right

A **cooled-down moment never exists.** A **suppressed rendition does** — invariant 11 and D14: *"a suppressed rendition is a record, not an absence"*, with a `suppression_reason`.

These are different mechanisms at different layers and the words collide. Do not reuse `SuppressionReason` here: its values (`muted`, `below_threshold`, `rate_limited`…) belong to `Rendition`, which is Selection's output. If M2 needs a reason at all, it is a different closed set and therefore a decision — ask.

**Worth noting, not necessarily building:** a cooled-down moment leaves no trace. P3 shows rejected *claims* with their reasons, and the analogous "moments we chose not to fire" has no record anywhere. That is a gap in the audit story rather than a bug, and closing it means a new row — so it is Kehinde's call, after the 27th.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The same `(signal, team, type)` cannot fire twice inside the cooldown window — assert with a constructed sequence, not by hoping a generated match contains one
- [ ] **A goal always fires**, even inside an active cooldown
- [ ] A signal oscillating across a threshold fires once, not once per crossing
- [ ] A genuinely new shift after the cooldown expires does fire — the test that catches a cooldown which never releases
- [ ] Moment count on the demo match stays inside M1's 30–60 band after suppression. If suppression drops it below 30, either the band or the cooldown is wrong, and the band was calibrated first
- [ ] Two matches in one process do not share suppression state
- [ ] Cooldown windows are config values, with no literal durations in the spotting code
- [ ] Deterministic on a fixed seed

## Do not

- Do not suppress goals.
- Do not reuse `Rendition.suppression_reason` or its enum.
- Do not fold this back into M1. The two-memory distinction is the reason it is separate.
- Do not add a new entity or field to record what was suppressed without asking.
