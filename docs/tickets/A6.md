# A6 · Evidence drawer in the UI

**Tier** T2 · **Est** 0.5 day · **Depends on** A3, L4 · **Phase** 6 Agents

## ⚠️ This is buildable now, and its dependency on A3 is looser than it looks

L7's claims already carry `evidence_ids`, and `Operand.evidence_ids` has `min_length=1` — **no claim exists without evidence.** So a drawer showing the events and readings behind a card can be built against what is already shipped. A3 is needed only to show the *verdict* alongside the evidence, not the evidence itself.

Worth knowing because of what this ticket is: the backlog says *"cheap, and judges will click it."* That understates it. **It is the only place the central product claim becomes checkable by a stranger.** Everything else in the submission asserts that nothing MatchLens says is unverifiable; this is where a judge tests it in two clicks.

Half a day, currently T2, and buildable today. **Worth considering promoting** — tiers are Kehinde's call.

## Goal

Any card opens to show the events and readings behind it (V7).

## What to build

- A card in the panel expands to show, per claim: the metric, the value, the window, and the cited rows.
- Cited rows rendered as what they are — an event with its clock and action, a reading with its signal, value and z-score. Enough that a reader can see the number came from somewhere.
- **Show the verdict when A3 exists**, per check, with its reason on a failure. Until then show the evidence and omit the verdict rather than implying one.
- Plain HTML and JS, no framework, no build step.
- Read-only. The drawer displays; it never recomputes, because a drawer that computes its own number could agree with itself while the pipeline is wrong.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every card opens, and every claim behind it lists its cited events and readings
- [ ] An `event_id` in the drawer corresponds to a real event, with a clock a viewer can match against the match clock
- [ ] A reading shows its signal, value and `z_match`, so "2.4σ above this match's baseline" is visible rather than asserted
- [ ] **No claim in the drawer has an empty evidence list** — the model makes it impossible, so this test documents the guarantee
- [ ] The drawer recomputes nothing
- [ ] With A3: per-check status and the reason for any failure. Without it: evidence only, no implied verdict
- [ ] It works on a suppressed lens's panel without implying the lens showed something
- [ ] No new dependency

## Definition of done

Criteria pass, green, status updated. **Screenshot one open drawer for the submission** — a goal card with its goal event, its shot quality and its build-up passes visible is the single most persuasive image in the pitch, because it is the claim being audited rather than repeated.

## Do not

- Do not show internal fields that are not evidence — no importance scores, no selection reasons, no lens ids.
- Do not recompute a value in the browser.
- Do not imply a verdict before A3 exists.
