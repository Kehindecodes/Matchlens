# R2 · Four MatchArchetype parameter sets

**Tier** T1 · **Est** 0.5 day · **Depends on** F3 · **Phase** Reference data — offline, runs in parallel

## Goal

Three more `ArchetypeParams` beside `COMEBACK`: **one-sided**, **tight contest**, **scrappy**. Each produces recognisably different matches from any seed.

## Why this is not just variety

Two things downstream depend on it, and both are load-bearing.

**Co-adaptation.** The data model says it directly: *"signals must be checked against several archetypes, or the simulator and the signals quietly co-adapt until the signals only work on the matches the generator happens to make."* One archetype means U3 is calibrated against a single match shape and nobody finds out until it meets a second one. P4 is the regression check and needs four to run against.

**U3's most important criterion lands here.** Under D24, U3 must show that **`pressing_intensity` dissociates from `possession_control`** on at least one archetype — that is the evidence for D10's orthogonal-basis claim, and it is the reason pressing survived the signal cut. A match where press and possession rise and fall together on every archetype proves nothing.

So one archetype must deliberately pair **low possession with high press for the same team**, sustained over a real spell. The natural home is `ONE_SIDED`: the dominated side sitting deep in shape but counter-pressing hard the moment it loses the ball. Without that, U3 cannot pass its own criterion and the orthogonality claim has no evidence behind it.

## What to build

In `matchlens/source/archetypes.py`, following `COMEBACK`'s shape — `momentum`, `home_press`, `away_press`, `goals`, `subs`, `red_cards`:

- **`ONE_SIDED`** — momentum pinned to one side for most of the clock. The dominant side on top throughout; **the dominated side's press stays high while its possession stays low** (see above). Two or three goals one way, a consolation optional.
- **`TIGHT`** — momentum oscillating around zero with low amplitude. Both sides pressing moderately. Few shots, low chance quality, goals late or not at all.
- **`SCRAPPY`** — momentum noisy and switching fast. Both presses high. Many fouls, turnovers and restarts, so possession sequences are short — this is the archetype that should make U1's mean-sequence-length assertion work hardest.

Register all four in `ARCHETYPES`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Four entries in `ARCHETYPES`, each a valid `ArchetypeParams` with schedules covering 0–100 minutes
- [x] **The archetypes separate statistically, not anecdotally.** Run N seeds each and assert the distributions of shot count, possession share, goal count and foul count differ by more than within-archetype seed variance. "They look different" is not the criterion
- [x] **Pressing does not simply track possession (Q17):** in every archetype, `possession_control` and `pressing_intensity` correlate weakly, |r| < 0.6, for both teams. The generator cannot sustain low possession with a high press for one team (Q17), so this is the claim the pitch makes
- [x] Final-score shapes differ across the four: a comeback, a one-way scoreline, a tight low-scoring game, and a scrappy one
- [x] `SCRAPPY` produces materially shorter possession sequences than `TIGHT`
- [x] Determinism preserved: every archetype × seed pair is byte-identical across runs (F3's property must survive)
- [x] **The archetype boundary has a test (D25):** nothing outside `matchlens/source/` and `tests/` imports `archetypes` or reads `Match.archetype_id` / `Match.seed`. Same shape as `test_nothing_outside_config_reads_os_environ` — walk the tree and assert the offender list is empty
- [x] All four run end to end through F3 with ~1,800 events and plausible in-play time

## Definition of done

Criteria pass, `ruff` and `pytest` green, status updated.

## Do not

- Do not encode moments, importance, or anything that judges what is interesting. An archetype says how a match is *driven* (D8, D12). The generator declaring what matters is the circularity this whole design exists to avoid.
- **Do not tune an archetype to make a signal fire.** That is co-adaptation in its purest form, and it is precisely what P4 exists to catch. If a signal only reads well after an archetype is adjusted to suit it, the signal is wrong.
- Do not add a fifth archetype. Four is what P4 needs and what the budget allows.
- Do not change `COMEBACK`. The demo video and the committed demo seed depend on it.

## Worth noting for later

`ONE_SIDED` is the archetype where **defensive line height** would have mattered most — a side dropping deep to protect a lead is the read D24 deferred. When line height is reinstated, this is the archetype to test it against.
