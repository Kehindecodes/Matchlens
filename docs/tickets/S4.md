# S4 · The skeleton walks

**Tier** T1 · **Est** 0.5 day · **Depends on** S2, S3 · **Phase** 1 Walking skeleton

## Goal

One hardcoded moment travels from Match Source through **every stage** and arrives in a browser as a card.

## Why this is the milestone that matters

This is the day the project stops being documents. After S4 there is always something to look at, and every later phase thickens a stage that already works rather than adding a stage that has never run.

It also forces the stages to meet on day 5 rather than day 15. The first time they meet is the first time they can fail together, and you want that early.

## What to build

- A wiring module — `apps/pipeline.py` — that runs the stages in order over the source stream.
- A hardcoded `Moment` injected by Spotting at a fixed clock (say 67:12, matching the prototype's reference moment).
- Stub content through Explanation → Verification → Personalization: a fixed `Claim`, a `Verification` with `verdict=pass`, one `Rendition` with real text.
- The rendition reaching Delivery and out over SSE to the page from S3.
- The page rendering it as a card with its clock and body.

Every stage must be **called**, even where it returns a constant. A stage skipped now is a stage that has never run when you get to its ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Starting the pipeline and opening the page produces a card in the browser at the right match clock
- [ ] A test asserts the end-to-end path: given a seed, a rendition arrives at a session with expected text
- [ ] All thirteen components appear in the call path — add a trace or a simple ordered log and assert each was entered
- [ ] Nothing is bypassed: no direct hop from Spotting to Delivery

## Definition of done

Criteria pass, green, status updated. Take a screenshot — it is the first real evidence the architecture works, and useful in the pitch.

## Do not

- Do not make the content good. It is a constant and that is correct for this ticket.
- Do not add the second or third lens yet. Phase 4.
- Do not skip Verification because the claim is hardcoded. The point is that the path exists.
