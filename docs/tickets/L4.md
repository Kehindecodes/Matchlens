# L4 · Three-panel UI on one clock, silent lens visibly idle

**Tier** T1 · **Est** 1 day · **Depends on** L3, S3 · **Phase** 4 Lenses ⭐

## Goal

One page, three panels, one match clock. When a lens shows nothing, **it reads as deliberately quiet rather than broken** (J3).

## This is the shot the demo video is built around

From Positioning: *"Said aloud, 'AI explains the match to you' sounds like the commentary feed. Shown, one panel sitting deliberately silent while another fires cannot be explained as one engine with three skins."* Everything else in the build exists to make this screen true. It is also the screen a judge spends the longest looking at.

## What to build

Extend `web/index.html` and `web/app.js` from the single panel S3/S4 built.

- **Three panels side by side**, each labelled with its lens name and audience, sharing one clock spine so a viewer can see the same instant in all three.
- **The idle state is the hard part.** A blank panel reads as a bug. It needs to show it is alive and listening: the clock advancing, the lens's last card still in place, and a quiet indicator that this lens is choosing not to speak. Do not use a spinner — a spinner means *loading*, which is the opposite of the message.
- Cards show the headline, body and the match clock they describe (`Card.display_at_clock`), which is what crosses the bus — `Rendition` carries no clock.
- Plain HTML and JS, no framework and no build step (pinned stack).

## ⚠️ Decide how the three panels are fed

A real viewer has **one** lens; this page shows three at once, so it is a demo view rather than a viewer view. Two shapes:

- **three sessions**, one per lens, three SSE connections — closest to production, and it exercises the per-session release queue three times over
- **one session carrying three lenses** — simpler, but it is a shape production never uses

Worth knowing: **P1, the producer view, is "largely the same component — build once"** per the backlog. Whatever you choose here is what P1 inherits, so choose for P1 as much as for the demo.

**Panels are fed by three sessions, one `EventSource` each (Q24: A).**

## Do not

- Do not add a chart library or a framework.
- Do not show internal fields on a card — no reasons, no ids, no importance scores. That is the producer view (P1).
- Do not let a panel reorder or retro-fill cards. Delivery releases in clock order and the page shows what it is given (invariant 8).
